# Spec — content distribution & asset policy for the cherokeelessons.org stand-up

Remote issue: https://github.com/CherokeeLanguage/CherokeeLessons/issues/30

## Problem

The site carries ~3.49 GB of untracked-but-site-required assets, and its distribution model is changing by developer decision (session 2026-11-11): app/game distribution moves to the itch.io channel (`cherokee-language.itch.io` — 4 projects live, verified from the public profile), and lesson media is already mirrored on the developer's YouTube channel (`@-cherokeelanguage7554` — all 21 dictation videos, 23 CLL-1 tracks, and 9 animal-names tracks identified there by stable title prefixes (track numbering) and duration correspondence, `research/youtube-video-id-map.txt`). Three page-less direct-URL surfaces from the private Language-Preservation-Technologies repos are slated for removal, the TTS demo audio and book TeX sources are untracked (so a fresh-clone build is incomplete), and the Google verification file is untracked. An executed stand-up gate (2026-11-11, shallow clone + pinned-zola build: 1,547 files / 0.48 GiB, exit 0) shows the fresh-clone build omits every untracked asset class; the same gate shows the planned Cloudflare Workers deployment (separate spec) is blocked by four tracked files over the platform's 25 MiB/file limit — the two app jars and two book PDFs; under the revised sequencing (2026-11-12, developer directive) the hosting spec CherokeeLessons#31 executes first and clears all four itself (deleting the jars, relocating the PDFs), with this spec's R-1 removing the jar page links and SC-2/SC-10 verifying the resulting end state. By developer decision (2026-11-11), itch.io is the only release platform: all pre-existing store links on the app pages are removed — the probe matrix (`research/store-link-probe.txt`) shows them predominantly dead (Google Play 404 ×5, Apple 404 ×2/200 ×1, Amazon 503 ×4; two still live — the animals Beta-testing link and the Syllabary Apple link — removed by policy), and the CLL-1 page's itch link arrives via the companion page-link spec (CherokeeLessons#32) after the cll1-gdx publish (CherokeeLanguage/cll1-gdx#5) delivers its itch project.

## Requirements

- **R-1 — App distribution via itch.io (itch-only).** The four itch-backed app pages SHALL reference their itch.io project URLs as the distribution target, replacing the self-hosted binary links in their actual forms — relative colocated links (`Animals-5.12.jar`, `Animals-5.12-release.apk`, `BoundPronouns-3.41.jar`, `BoundPronouns-3.41-release.apk`, `NumbersMaze.x86_64`, `NumbersMaze-mac.zip`, `NumbersMaze-x86_64.exe`) and root-absolute `/downloads/…jar` links; the `/HTML-Games/NumbersMaze/` link is replaced by the itch target; the commented-out wrong NumbersMaze store links SHALL be removed; the two tracked desktop jars' page links SHALL be removed — the tracked jar files themselves are deleted by CherokeeLessons#31's R-9 (executes first under the revised sequencing, 2026-11-12); the Syllabary page gains its itch link. Per the developer's itch-only decision ("only itch-io is the new release platform", 2026-11-11), ALL store links on the five app pages SHALL be removed — every `play.google.com`, `apps.apple.com`, and `amazon.com` link, including the animals page's Beta-testing link and the Syllabary page's Apple link (the only two probe-live store links — removed by policy, not because dead; the probe matrix is supporting evidence). The CLL-1 page's itch link is added by the companion page-link spec CherokeeLessons#32, which depends on the cll1-gdx publish (CherokeeLanguage/cll1-gdx#5) delivering the itch project; during this spec's window that page carries no distribution links. With the page rewrites, the untracked colocated binaries in the three app dirs that carry them (animals: 2 files; bound-pronouns: 6; cherokee-numbers-maze: 3 — 11 files, ~470 MB; `syllabary-app.md` has no colocated binaries) and the untracked `static/HTML-Games/NumbersMaze` web build (7 files, ~44 MB) — together the ~514 MB "already-on-itch" retirement class of the work record — SHALL be deleted from the working tree (no repo change — untracked).
- **R-2 — Lesson media via YouTube embeds.** The 14 dictation pages SHALL switch their 20 `{{ video(video="Dictation …mp4") }}` embeds, and the CLL-1 audio page (23) and animal-names audio page (9) their `{{audio(file=…)}}` embeds, to `{{ youtube(id=…) }}` using the verified mapping (`research/youtube-video-id-map.txt`); page→video assignment derives from each page's current media references; the untracked mp3/mp4 sources of these classes are deleted from the working tree. The CLL-1 page's `cll1-v3-audio.zip` download link SHALL be removed with the embeds — the link is dead in every fresh-clone/CI build (the zip is untracked and never deploys; the executed gate's build omitted it), so keeping it would ship a dead link on the platform of record; the zip file itself stays untracked on disk and its disposition is deferred (Out of scope). The two tracked page-generator scripts `content/lesson-audio/cherokee-language-lessons-1---audio-exercises/process_end_notes.py` and `content/lesson-audio/animal-names/process_end_notes.py` SHALL be deleted: they regenerate the old embed form (and, for CLL-1, the dead zip link) from the now-deleted local media, the build serves them verbatim as non-markdown content files (shipping the dead-link text), and re-running them post-migration would overwrite the migrated pages and fail on the deleted mp3s — tracked, so history-recoverable.
- **R-3 — LPT surfaces removed.** The three untracked directories SHALL be deleted from the working tree; the two vestigial tracked ignore artifacts SHALL be deleted.
- **R-4 — Track the TTS demo audio.** The 45 untracked demo mp3s (24 + 9 + 8 TTS, plus the 4 espeak clips — same demo-page class, inclusion flagged to the developer in session) SHALL be tracked via per-dir `.gitignore` negation (`!*.mp3`), the repo's own established pattern (`tacotron2-and-cherokee-tts/.gitignore`): the tracked `Animals*.mp3` rule in `cherokee-animal-names-tts-demo-audio-1/.gitignore` is replaced, and new `.gitignore` files carrying `!*.mp3` are created in the dirs that have none (verified at plan time via `git check-ignore` — the other two TTS dirs have none today). Both 2021-05 generations of the animal TTS set are kept (the tracked `exercises.txt` references the 05-29 set while `index.md` embeds 05-31 — a recorded drift this spec preserves rather than resolves).
- **R-5 — Track the book TeX sources.** The 3 `.tex` files and their 3 `.md` conversions SHALL be tracked by removing the `tex-source/.gitignore` rules.
- **R-6 — Track the Google verification file.** `content/google593a568777046d5c.html` SHALL be tracked (the `content/.gitignore` google rules removed) — required for Search Console verification on the new host and consistent with R-4/R-5's reproducibility intent.
- **R-7 — Ebook distribution consolidated to the site.** The developer directive of record (2026-11-12: "only itch.io is the platform for distribution going forwards. the ebook is now only available via cherokeelessons.org.") names the ebook surface: the external ebook-store links SHALL be removed from the book pages — every `smashwords.com` ePub link and every `amazon.com` Kindle link across the 15 `content/books/` pages (26 link lines, measured in Provenance) — leaving the on-site free-PDF direct downloads as the only ebook channel. The tracked scaffold `content/books/new-page.sh` SHALL equally lose its two template store-link lines — they re-create external ebook-store links on every scaffolded page (a standing regression vector). Alternatives weighed: keeping any store links — rejected (the directive's "only" clause; the same removal shape as R-1's store-link policy, applied to the ebook channel).

## Approach

Follow the developer's distribution decisions of record: itch.io for apps, YouTube for the mirrored lesson media (embeds via the existing `{{ youtube() }}` shortcode — first content use 2021-02, shortcode file tracked since 2023-05), removal for the page-less LPT surfaces, tracking for site-required sources, site-only ebook distribution (external ebook-store links removed from the book pages and the scaffold). Alternatives weighed: converting the legacy `.htaccess` rules — rejected (developer: legacy redirects discarded); keeping self-hosted app binaries alongside itch — rejected (the migration's purpose is retiring self-hosted distribution, and the platform's 25 MiB limit makes two of them un-deployable regardless); re-encoding the lesson media locally instead of YouTube embeds — rejected (the developer's own channel already hosts all 53 items verbatim). Keeping any store links (live or dead) alongside itch — rejected (the developer's itch-only decision; the pre-existing links are predominantly dead — probe matrix).
